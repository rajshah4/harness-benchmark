# Visual and Educational Rubric

Use this rubric after the deterministic behavior checker. Score each dimension from 0 to 2 for a total of 20 points.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scientific sequence | Missing or misleading | Mostly correct | Correct, clear, and well explained |
| Materials | Several missing or unrecognizable | Present but generic | Complete, recognizable, and thoughtfully illustrated |
| Direct manipulation | Static or broken | Basic movement | Smooth, precise movement with useful layering |
| Safety and recovery | Unsafe states or dead ends | Some guardrails | Clear prevention, feedback, and friendly recovery |
| Educational enhancement | None | Decorative or shallow | Useful microscope, growth, quiz, or equivalent feature |
| Information design | Confusing or redundant | Understandable | Focused hierarchy with no unnecessary repetition |
| Visual craft | Unfinished | Competent | Professional, coherent, and presentation ready |
| Feedback and motion | Absent or distracting | Functional | Smooth and meaningfully reinforces learning |
| Responsive behavior | Breaks at phone size | Usable with compromises | Intentionally designed across viewport sizes |
| Overall experience | Prototype | Demonstrable | Memorable workshop-quality learning experience |

Record a short note for every score. Reviewers should inspect the initial state, one invalid action, the full successful sequence, the completed state, and a phone-sized view.
